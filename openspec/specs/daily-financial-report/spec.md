# Daily LangGraph financial report

## Requirement: immutable advisory evidence for each requested approach

The daily operator workflow MUST invoke the deployed LangGraph runtime for PPO,
minimum variance, mean variance and scenario CVaR. It MUST retain exact hosted
payloads, skill identity, claim checks, numerical evidence and immutable decision
IDs in private per-attempt receipts. The same saved paper portfolio, revision,
approved snapshot and completed-session date MUST be verified in returned records
for controlled output comparisons. Current-default calls preserve morning decision
cutoffs; historical explicit dates MUST NOT silently backdate morning news. Classical optimizer settings MUST be explicit and identical across
classical families; differences in PPO's frozen feasible set, horizon and objective
MUST be disclosed. An unavailable method or incompatible requested universe MUST
remain an explicit readiness failure, never replaced with another policy.

## Requirement: three explanation families

The report MUST ask the LangGraph tool path for frozen recommendation rationale,
actual compatible distinct-market-date plan changes where available, and observed
realization/horizon discrepancies plus dated public context. It MUST also request
cross-method comparisons between issued decisions on the same snapshot and holdings.
Missing prior decisions or forward observations MUST be reported as unavailable.
Cross-method comparison MUST not be presented as causal feature attribution or
proof of a superior model. An issued target MUST never become an executed holding
through report generation.

## Requirement: market and news readiness

A daily 09:45 America/New_York operator timer MUST use the pinned XNYS session
calendar and compare served prices with its latest completed session. Regular US
equity open is 09:30; 09:00 retrieval is premarket. Sundays and exchange holidays
MUST produce clearly labeled latest completed-session reports with no claim of a
new market session. The report MUST inspect the daily news collector's immutable
completion marker and preserve profile delivery, central publication, availability
and training eligibility separately from actual model feature consumption.
Clock time alone MUST NOT establish freshness. Stale prices, missing current-session
news publication, unsupported assets or unconsumed required news MUST yield partial
readiness while retaining available advisory outputs.

## Requirement: unattended read-only operation

The operator MAY authenticate with the existing read-only CI machine client. It
MUST NOT impersonate a human, reset credentials, confirm decisions, change holdings,
place orders, launch training or promote models. Configuration, credentials and
portfolio-bearing reports MUST remain private. Hosted transport/authentication
failure MUST generate a durable readiness result without exposing credentials.

## Requirement: actual numeric news provenance

When the served policy consumes GDELT numeric metadata, the report/runtime MUST
state its feature protocol, actual UTC archive source day, source and central
availability, and current decision cutoff separately from latest completed prices.
Derived source lag MUST use the returned timestamps. A local collector marker MUST
NOT establish central availability. Daily machine-metadata archives MUST NOT be
presented as same-morning breaking headlines or calibrated stock sentiment; VOO
Vanguard mentions MUST NOT be described as a constituent-weighted news basket.
Separately collected paid research headlines retain their own rights and readiness.
