## Why

Daily attribution alone cannot evaluate a sequential policy over its intended objective horizon. The hosted agent must distinguish short observations from strategy evidence and support a bounded research loop that preserves decisions and experiment lineage.

## What Changes

- Present declared objectives, evaluation windows, frozen sequential replay, controls and evidence limitations through the existing portfolio evaluation workflow.
- Package the same investigation instructions for hosted and direct MCP use.
- Add a recursive improvement conversation path that reviews evidence, estimates or confirms bounded sandbox work, resumes persisted cycles and proposes changes for human review.
- Expose Qwen swarm and Jev benchmark capability status without substituting unavailable implementations or launching unapproved compute.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `horizon-and-recursive-investigation`: Objective-aware portfolio review and bounded recursive research conversations.

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact

Additive model evidence and one MCP operation; beta deployment only, existing paid-research safeguards, immutable activity audit and original paper holdings preserved.
