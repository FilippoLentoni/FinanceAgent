# Design

Use the existing isolated management/execution roles and active embedded wallet. A separate canonical local ledger records each hourly asset slot once and reserves the entire quoted amount before calling ProcessPayment. Unknown signing or delivery outcomes never release reservations or trigger automatic retries. BlockRun Exa news is fixed to one HTTPS endpoint and reviewed payment recipient, exact Base mainnet Circle USDC, at most USD 0.03 per request. Validate the HTTP 402 challenge, then sign only its payment requirements through AgentCore. Sessions last 15 minutes and are capped at USD 0.03; cumulative reservations across all sessions remain <= USD 10. Start the seven-day period only after successful funding preflight. Local disk accounting is suitable only for one operator host; hosted/distributed acquisition is outside this change.

No balance was found on Base USDC during the live preflight. Collection must remain unstarted until the wallet owner funds the active instrument. A received response is research evidence, not certified quality, licensing, settlement or a policy improvement. Preserve first-seen availability and original provider timestamps; prohibit historical backfill claims.
