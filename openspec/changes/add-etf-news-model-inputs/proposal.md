# Proposal

## Why
The user prefers ETF portfolio planning and has authorized a cumulative USD 10 paid research trial. A paid BTC search proved delivery, but the fixed crypto procurement runner neither collects ETF news nor stores it centrally or exposes eligible model inputs.

## What Changes
- Add a fixed ETF market-news query profile to the existing single-host ledger without resetting the USD 10 cap or seven-day expiry.
- Store signed-payment and delivery evidence privately and publish sanitized immutable ETF news metadata through the platform ingestion API.
- Schedule one shared market-news query per hourly slot in regular US market sessions, stopping on expiry, budget or insufficient funding.
- Expose ETF input/provenance and model readiness through LangGraph; distinguish research-only metadata from training rights and quarantine unknown material.
- Verify hosted read-only tools and matched model-input experiments without claiming improved returns before a valid study exists.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `etf-news-conversation`: Bounded ETF news procurement, central evidence publication and conversations about eligible model inputs.
### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
FinanceAgent operator procurement/timer, MCP schema registration, ETF model-input skill, tests and paper evidence. Coordinated with FinancialPlanning, FinanceModel and FinanceLambdasTool under add-etf-news-model-inputs. No additional budget, automatic live ETF orders or unauthorized training spend.
