# Versioned paper portfolio lifecycle

Recommend using `recommend_portfolio` (selected PPO/policy) or `recommend_classical_portfolio` (traditional optimizer). Both use pre-ingested approved completed market data and the latest saved holdings revision; neither changes holdings. Save the issued `decision_id`, market snapshot and source revision, and show all assets plus cash.

For acceptance or rejection, re-read `get_portfolio_decision` by exact issued decision ID. Present its strategy, market date, proposed changes, source revision and simulated reference-price execution/cost semantics. Obtain explicit human approval of the exact resolution before calling `resolve_portfolio_decision` with action, expected_revision, confirmed_by_user=true and an idempotency key. The remote MCP must receive the user's access token in the transport header X-Finplan-User-Token; never put tokens or caller groups into tool arguments. A rejection leaves holdings unchanged. Acceptance saves fractional simulated fills and one new holdings revision. Never call a broker or claim these are live fills. A stale revision requires a new recommendation; repeated acceptance must never apply twice.

For why, retrieve `get_portfolio_decision` then call `explain_portfolio_decision` with its stored portfolio and decision IDs. Traditional evidence may include objective counterfactuals and Shapley. PPO diagnostics describe learned target changes; do not invent neural feature attribution or news causality. Explanations use frozen evidence even after the saved holdings or selected policy changes.

For plan over plan, use `list_portfolio_decisions` to discover actual issued IDs and compare with `compare_portfolio_decisions`. For yesterday/today require distinct recorded market dates; do not regenerate or invent yesterday's recommendation. For outcomes use `evaluate_portfolio_decision`, distinguish accepted paper accounting from simulated target allocation, and read its `horizon_evaluation` before judging the strategy. Its declared objective, frozen sequential replay, same-cost controls, partial windows and limitations are separate from daily P&L and first-allocation hold. No separate forecast is needed for these retrospective comparisons. Use `research_market_events` for dated context only. Feed retained discrepancy analyses and acceptance/rejection outcomes into bounded research review; no automatic paid launch or strategy activation.

For historical postmortems, fetch `get_portfolio_history`, `list_portfolio_decisions` and `list_market_snapshots` (default latest three). Preserve joins from holdings revision through decision to input snapshot, algorithm and execution. Use `list_agent_activity` for independently archived tool/agent receipts, and `record_agent_activity` for completed investigation narratives, exact skill version/checksum and tool evidence references. Credentials and download grants must be redacted. Missing historical data must be explicit.
