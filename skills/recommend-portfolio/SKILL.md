# recommend-portfolio

When asked how to invest today, plan a portfolio, rebalance, or buy/sell Google, Nvidia or another supported instrument, use the read-only recommend_portfolio MCP tool. For the ordinary recommendation call it with {}. The service loads the persisted beta paper portfolio, marks its saved quantities and cash at the latest approved completed market session, and runs the selected frozen strategy. Do not ask the user to repeat holdings for this saved-paper workflow. Disclose that these are the saved paper holdings, not assumed actual brokerage positions.

For explicitly supplied actual/paper state, preserve the user's input and collect missing snapshot, completed session, weights, cash weight, portfolio value and high watermark. Never replace incomplete explicitly supplied actual holdings with the saved paper book. Previously recommended target weights do not prove executed positions. Missing saved state or missing approved prices must be reported rather than invented. Do not refresh data, initialize holdings or create a job automatically.

The strategy can be a trained policy or classical optimizer. Use the tool's reported strategy identifier; never assume PPO. Return every instrument and cash target, current and target fractional shares, signed buy/sell share and value changes, reference prices and price dates, decision session, saved portfolio identity/revision, exact policy/data provenance and limitations. Negative changes mean selling; positive changes mean buying. Quantities are indicative at the reference close before execution costs, rounding and price movement. Decisions made after a completed close apply to the next session. The complete producer recommendation remains available in answer.recommendation.

Recommendation narration is deterministic from the tool result. Explain that the proposed trades move current holdings toward constrained model targets. Do not invent a neural causal explanation, claim a particular news story caused a policy action, omit cash, or call the portfolio fully invested when cash is present. A policy critic or historical benchmark is not a calibrated return forecast.

Recommendations never execute trades, update the saved positions, publish plans, promote strategies, retrain networks or change risk preferences. Asking again must read and revalue saved positions; a prior recommendation does not become an executed position. Explain observed performance against the exact published plan using explain-performance and recorded evidence. Unknown causes remain unknown.

For "why is this your recommendation?" or an explanation follow-up, use the most recent successful recommend_portfolio tool result as a replay reference, not as current evidence. Re-call recommend_portfolio with its original approved snapshot/date and saved portfolio_id, or the unchanged original explicit holdings request. Also call query_market_data for that same snapshot and completed session. Verify the policy artifact/configuration, portfolio-state revision and allocations still match the referenced recommendation. If the saved book, policy or evidence changed or cannot be read, report that the original recommendation cannot be reproduced; do not substitute a new recommendation silently. With no successful prior recommendation, request that context first.

Explain which learned targets are above or below the current holdings and how the proposed trades bridge that difference. These are state-conditioned model targets, not fixed weights chosen by the user. This is a mechanical explanation of policy output, not feature-level attribution or a claim that a particular news item caused an action. The hosted agent reports the applied skill's name, version and instruction checksum. Direct MCP clients discover this same full recipe in recommend_portfolio's tool description; native MCP prompt/resource lists need not contain the skill.
